Democrats Win Control of US Senate in Midterm Elections, Projected to Take Chamber from Republicans

politics · 4 Nov 2026 · 2 min read · By World News Today (World Desk)

Decision Desk HQ and NBC projected a Democratic Senate majority; Democrats had already been projected to win the House earlier in the evening.

Democrats have captured control of the US Senate in Tuesday's midterm elections, Decision Desk HQ and NBC projected, completing a Democratic takeover of Congress after the party had already been projected to win a majority in the House of Representatives earlier in the evening.

Democrats flipped Senate seats in South Carolina, North Carolina, Texas, Ohio, Kansas and Iowa, according to USA Today. Democrats needed to pick up four seats to take control of the chamber.

Among the projected winners, Adam Hamilton is projected to be the first Democrat to win a Kansas Senate seat since 1932, and James Talarico is the first Democrat to win a Texas Senate seat since 1988.

A competitive Senate race in Michigan, where Abdul El-Sayed is looking to keep the seat in Democratic hands, remains uncalled.

Retaking the Senate will give Democrats legislative leverage in shaping Washington's agenda, USA Today reported. They are likely to pursue reversing Medicaid and food stamp cuts, reasserting Congress's role on foreign trade policy, and seeking bipartisan agreement on banning lawmakers from trading stocks.

The immediate showdown, however, will likely center on the war in Iran, and could see Democrats directing President Donald Trump to seek congressional authorization to continue or pulling funding to end the conflict. Trump will also have a harder time installing Cabinet and judicial nominees, including for another potential Supreme Court vacancy, should one arise.
