Trump Names Stephen Miller as New White House Chief of Staff, Replacing Susie Wiles

politics · 6 Nov 2026 · 2 min read · By World News Today (World Desk)

Miller, 41, the architect of the president's hardline immigration agenda, takes over on Monday; Wiles, who resigned on Thursday, moves to Trump's political operation.

President Donald Trump said on Friday he was promoting deputy chief of staff Stephen Miller to become White House chief of staff, with current chief of staff Susie Wiles moving over to his political operation. The change takes effect on Monday, November 9.

In a post on Truth Social, Trump called Miller "one of my most trusted advisors" and praised Wiles for doing "an outstanding job". He wrote: "I am pleased to announce that Mr. Stephen Miller, the current Deputy Chief of Staff of the Trump Administration, and one of my most trusted advisors, will become the Chief of Staff starting Monday, November 9, 2026."

Wiles submitted her resignation on Thursday evening, according to people familiar with the matter, and said in a statement on Friday that it was her last day as White House chief of staff. She was the first woman to hold the post and had served since the start of Trump's second term, after leading his successful 2024 presidential campaign.

Miller, 41, has served as deputy chief of staff for policy and Homeland Security adviser since January 2025, and has been the architect of the president's hardline immigration agenda, including the deportation policies at the centre of his second term. He is one of Trump's longest-serving and most trusted advisers, dating back to his first campaign.

The move is the first major personnel shuffle since Tuesday's midterm elections, in which Democrats won control of both the House of Representatives and the Senate, dealing Republicans their worst midterm defeat since the Watergate era.

Miller said on X that it had been "the privilege of my lifetime" to serve on Trump's team from day one, adding that he was "honored beyond words to serve as his White House Chief of Staff."
