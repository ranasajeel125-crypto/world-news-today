Oleshky Evacuees Arrive in Belarus After Buses, Flights in Rare Joint Operation

world · 31 Oct 2026 · 2 min read · By World News Today (World Desk)

The evacuees travelled by bus via Crimea to Anapa in Russia before flying to Belarus, Belta said.

Three planes carrying residents evacuated from the Russian-controlled Ukrainian town of Oleshky landed at Gomel airport in Belarus, the Belta state news agency reported on Saturday.

The evacuees, wishing to leave for Ukraine, travelled by bus via the Crimean peninsula to the Russian town of Anapa before flying to Belarus, and will proceed to Ukraine via a border crossing, Belta said.

Starving and exhausted residents were evacuated by bus on Friday as part of a joint relief operation by the opposing sides, the first of its kind in four years and eight months of war, Reuters reported.

The evacuation began on Friday under a deal that Reuters reported US first lady Melania Trump was involved in brokering, with the additional involvement of Pope Leo. Both Moscow and Kyiv are trying to use the operation to show US President Donald Trump they are serious about his efforts to secure an overall peace settlement, Reuters reported.

The number of residents leaving Oleshky remains unclear: Kyiv's prime minister, Sergiy Koretskyi, said on Friday that there were no exact figures at the moment, retracting an earlier remark to parliament that 527 people were planning to leave. He had said the evacuation route would lead through Russia to Belarus, from where residents would enter Ukraine through the Kyiv and Chernihiv regions.

The International Committee of the Red Cross said on Friday its teams had not been asked to provide support but stood ready to help within its mandate if requested, while the UN humanitarian office said it had not received a request to help but was closely monitoring the situation.

Source: Reuters
