Five Shot in Washington DC Gym Attack Near White House; Suspect Barricaded

world · 12 Nov 2026 · 3 min read · By World News Today (World Desk)

Police said the shooting was reported just before 8 a.m. at 15th and P Streets NW in Logan Circle; the gunman was barricaded in a building above the gym and had not been taken into custody.

Five people were shot on Thursday morning at and near a VIDA Fitness gym in Washington DC’s Logan Circle neighbourhood, about a mile from the White House, police said, and the suspect barricaded himself in a building above the gym as officers surrounded the area.

Metropolitan Police Department spokesman Officer Nathan Clarke said five people were shot and that their conditions were not yet known. NBC4 Washington reported that four people were injured and one had died, though police had not confirmed the victims’ conditions.

The shooting was reported just before 8 a.m. local time near the intersection of 15th and P Streets NW, a busy block that also holds a Whole Foods supermarket, restaurants, apartments and St. Luke’s Episcopal Church. At least one victim was found inside the gym, Clarke said, but not all five were inside.

Police said the suspect was barricaded and not in custody, and asked people to stay indoors and away from the 1500 block of 15th Street NW. A shelter-in-place order was issued for the area, nearby schools were placed on lockdown and streets around 15th and P Streets and 15th and Q Streets were shut down.

Police issued an alert describing the suspect as a heavyset white man wearing grey shorts and carrying a grey and black gun. The Metropolitan Police Department’s Emergency Response Team was on the scene, and the FBI, the Bureau of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms and Explosives, DC Fire, Metro Transit Police, Homeland Security and the National Guard were among the agencies responding.

The situation was still active on Thursday morning, with the suspect’s identity and motive not yet publicly confirmed by police.
