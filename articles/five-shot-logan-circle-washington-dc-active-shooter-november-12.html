Five Shot in Washington DC Gym Attack Near White House; Suspect Barricaded

world · 12 Nov 2026 · 6 min read · By World News Today (World Desk)

VIDA Fitness in Logan Circle sits about a mile from the White House; police closed surrounding streets and placed nearby schools on lockdown.

Five people were shot on Thursday morning at and near a VIDA Fitness gym in Washington DC's Logan Circle neighbourhood, about a mile from the White House, police said, and the suspect barricaded himself in a building above the gym as officers surrounded the area.

The shooting was reported just before 8 a.m. local time near the intersection of 15th and P Streets NW, a busy block that also holds a Whole Foods supermarket, restaurants, apartments and St. Luke's Episcopal Church. Metropolitan Police Department spokesman Officer Nathan Clarke said five people were shot and that their conditions were not yet known. NBC4 Washington reported that four people were injured and one had died, though police had not confirmed the victims' conditions at the time. The man killed was James Stuart, 46, the manager of the building in which the suspect lived. He was doing his morning workout at the gym when he was shot. A witness, James Spellman, who was in the gym at the time, said there were about 50 people there. "Within seconds, there was a man who fell to the ground, and a pool of blood came out," Spellman said. "One gym member rushed to help him. He was instantly dead. He had been shot in the neck." Spellman said he and other gym members ran out of the gym and heard more gunfire in the area of 15th Street — about two shots fired in the gym and about five or six fired outside. Another witness said she had to step over the dead victim to leave the gym before hiding in a nearby bagel store as more gunshots rang out.

Police later said a sixth person had been shot, bringing the toll to one person killed and five wounded. In an updated statement, the Metropolitan Police Department said investigators had found a sixth victim, a woman, after officers initially located five other victims. She was conscious and breathing with a non-life-threatening gunshot graze wound, and had self-treated the wound. Two women and two men were taken to hospital, all conscious and breathing; one of the wounded was in critical condition, while the others did not have life-threatening injuries. Some of the wounded had been working at a construction site across the street. Police have not detailed the circumstances in which the sixth victim was shot. The shootings unfolded in less than five minutes, according to interim police chief Jeffery Carroll.

Police said the suspect was barricaded and not in custody, and asked people to stay indoors and away from the 1500 block of 15th Street NW. A shelter-in-place order was issued for the area, nearby schools were placed on lockdown and streets around 15th and P Streets and 15th and Q Streets were shut down. Police issued an alert describing the suspect as a heavyset white man wearing grey shorts and carrying a grey and black gun. The Metropolitan Police Department's Emergency Response Team was on the scene, and the FBI, the Bureau of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms and Explosives, DC Fire, Metro Transit Police, Homeland Security and the National Guard were among the agencies responding. Police later established a reunification centre for residents affected by the incident at the Stead Park Recreation Center, at 1625 P Street NW.

The suspect was identified as Matt Wade Lambert, a 55-year-old doctor and resident of the building. Police believe Lambert targeted Stuart, then went back to his apartment on the seventh floor and fired on people below from his patio balcony. Cameras inside the gym showed Lambert waited for more than an hour for Stuart to arrive before he approached and shot him from behind. Evidence suggests Lambert "plotted" the attack, Chief Carroll said. Lambert then barricaded himself inside his apartment, where he talked to police negotiators over the phone for about an hour, with snipers keeping watch from nearby rooftops. At one point, he shot at police through the barricaded door; police say he fired at least twice through the door while officers were outside, but no officers were injured. He also broke the gas line connected to his fireplace and started a fire, which the building's sprinkler system contained. Police later found several hundred rounds of ammunition in the apartment, along with the broken gas line and fire accelerants.

About an hour after negotiations began, police heard gunfire over the phone, and Lambert stopped talking. When tactical officers forced their way into the apartment, they found him dead from an apparent self-inflicted gunshot wound. He was found just before 11 a.m. Police said inside the apartment they found a shotgun, a rifle and a handgun, all of which had been used in the shooting.

The shooting appeared to have stemmed from a "conflict between him and the homeowners association of the building", Chief Carroll said. Lambert's problems began more than two years ago, when he claimed in court filings that his million-dollar penthouse had become unlivable. He bought the two-bedroom penthouse condo in the Logan Circle neighbourhood for 1,095,000 dollars in 2016. For more than a year, mould grew along his walls, water saturated the ceiling and fire suppression system pipes leaked, Lambert alleged in a March 2025 lawsuit against his homeowners' association. According to court records cited by the Associated Press, Lambert had filed a lawsuit alleging that he had not been able to enjoy his penthouse since 2024 because of mould, moisture and water problems that began after work had been done on the building's exterior. The dispute spiralled into an expensive, years-long court battle over who, if anyone, was responsible for the damage, and the lawsuit was ongoing when Lambert killed Stuart. Lambert appeared to have shared details of the dispute, including photos and an engineering report, on an X account called OfMiceandMold. His lawyer, Joel Braithwaite, who represented him in the civil lawsuit, said Lambert was "in great spirits" but that litigation "wears on you", and said he was at a loss. Lambert had also posted exchanges with Stuart on the account. Police believe the disagreement may be connected to the shooting, but the precise motive remains under investigation.

VIDA Fitness, a gay-owned gym that caters to the LGBT community, temporarily shut its Logan Circle facility and announced that it would remain closed until further notice. Authorities cleared the building where the shooting occurred, along with a nearby apartment building. The investigation into the shooting and the circumstances surrounding the suspect's reported apartment-related conflict remains ongoing, including how the firearms were obtained, the sequence of events and whether the other wounded victims were specifically targeted.
