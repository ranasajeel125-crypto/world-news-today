First Day of Abu Dhabi Peace Talks ‘Constructive’, Zelensky Says

world · 10 Nov 2026 · 3 min read · By World News Today (World Desk)

The delegations discussed humanitarian steps, including prisoner exchanges and the return of Ukrainian civilians and children, as well as security guarantees for Ukraine; the talks resume for a second day on Wednesday.

Russia and Ukraine held their first US-mediated talks in nine months on ending their war on Tuesday, with US Secretary of State Marco Rubio calling the meeting “very productive” and Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky saying the session was “constructive and quite substantive”.

The session in Abu Dhabi, the capital of the United Arab Emirates, lasted four hours, and another is scheduled for a second day on Wednesday. It is the first trilateral meeting since February, with the United States represented by President Donald Trump’s son-in-law Jared Kushner and envoy Steve Witkoff. Neither Mr Rubio nor Mr Zelensky attended the talks.

Mr Zelensky said the talks were largely about humanitarian issues, including prisoner exchanges and returning Ukrainian civilians in captivity, as well as children taken from the country. Security guarantees for Ukraine were also discussed, he added during an appearance at the University of Copenhagen in Denmark.

A senior Ukrainian official, speaking on condition of anonymity, said Ukraine and Russia had talked about a large-scale prisoner swap, although the details will be discussed in the coming days.

Mr Kushner said he believed there were “some good ideas how to bridge” the differences between the two sides. Mr Rubio, speaking in Paris at a meeting of the Group of Seven, urged patience: “There’s a lot of work to be done in that regard. It’s not like something that’s on the verge of being solved in the next 24 hours or anything like that.”

The two sides remain far apart on the key elements of any deal. Russian President Vladimir Putin wants Ukraine to pull its troops back from four regions of Ukraine that Moscow annexed in September 2022 but has never fully captured, and demands that Kyiv abandon its bid to join Nato and accept strict limits on its military. Mr Zelensky rejects ceding territory and any limits on Ukraine’s military or its arrangements with Western partners, which Kyiv sees as essential security guarantees.

There is no sign the fighting is abating. Russia launched 162 drones and a ballistic missile at Ukraine overnight, Ukraine’s air force said, while Russia’s Defence Ministry said its forces had downed 155 Ukrainian drones overnight in eight Russian regions, as well as over Crimea.
