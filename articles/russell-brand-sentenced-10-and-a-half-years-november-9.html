Russell Brand Jailed for 10-and-a-Half Years for Rape and Sexual Assault

world · 9 Nov 2026 · 3 min read · By World News Today (World Desk)

The judge called Brand a sexual predator who believed fame meant he could act as he liked; he must serve two-thirds of the sentence before parole.

British comedian and actor Russell Brand was jailed for 10-and-a-half years on Monday after being convicted of raping one woman and sexually assaulting three more around two decades ago.

The 51-year-old stood in the dock at London's Southwark Crown Court as Judge Joel Bennathan sentenced him to 10 years and six months — nine years for the rape and 18 months for the three sexual assaults — and told him he must serve two-thirds of the sentence before becoming eligible for parole.

The judge described Brand as a sexual predator who believed "fame meant you could act how you liked, when you liked", and condemned what he called his "staggering arrogance".

Brand was found guilty on Thursday, 5 November, after a four-week trial, of one count of rape and three counts of sexual assault, for offences committed between 2004 and 2009. He was acquitted of two further counts of rape and one count of attempted indecent assault. He had denied all the charges.

The rape was of a 21-year-old fan in 2009 at his London home, days after meeting her at one of his stand-up shows at the Hammersmith Apollo in west London. The three sexual assaults were of women who worked in television and radio.

Brand, who rose to fame in the early 2000s hosting television programmes and his own BBC radio show before becoming a Hollywood film star, was remanded in custody after the verdicts and spent the weekend at HMP Wandsworth before being brought back to court on Monday to be sentenced.

London's Metropolitan Police said it is continuing to investigate two further allegations of rape against Brand, the BBC reported.
