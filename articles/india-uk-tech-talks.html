India and UK Explore Deeper Ties in Space, Nuclear Energy and AI

technology · 24 Oct 2026 · 3 min read · By Tom Becker (Technology Editor)

India and the United Kingdom have held bilateral talks to strengthen cooperation in space, civil nuclear energy and emerging technologies, India's Ministry of Science & Technology announced. The discussions, held on 12 October, focused on research partnerships, the use of small modular reactors, and connecting India's 800 space-technology startups with British companies.

Both nations also explored collaboration in artificial intelligence, quantum technologies and medical research, including nuclear medicine and biomedical research. The Indian delegation highlighted how space-sector reforms have opened the industry to private companies and startups.

The talks sit under India's SHANTI Act of 2025, which reshaped the country's nuclear-energy framework, and signal a push to translate the India–UK strategic partnership into concrete industrial and research outcomes rather than communiqués.

Officials said the initiatives' start dates would be announced separately — but the breadth of the agenda, spanning satellites to small reactors, makes this one of the most substantive technology engagements between the two countries since the 2030 Roadmap.
