Russia Launches Massive Overnight Strike on Kyiv, Hitting Northern Bridge and Energy Sites

world · 7 Nov 2026 · 3 min read · By World News Today (World Desk)

Russia's defence ministry said the strikes hit the Northern Bridge and the Meridian plant; Ukrainian officials said one person was killed in the Kyiv region and six people were injured in the capital.

Russia said its forces carried out a massive overnight strike on what it described as targets in Ukraine's military-industrial sector and energy and transport infrastructure in Kyiv and the surrounding region, Reuters reported.

Russia's defence ministry said the strikes hit the Northern Bridge — which it said was being used to transport weapons, military equipment and vehicles for the Ukrainian armed forces — and the Meridian plant, which Russia says produces Flamingo cruise missiles.

The ministry also said the strikes damaged several energy and industrial facilities in the Kyiv region, including a power plant, an electricity substation and a munitions manufacturing facility. It added that Russian forces struck an electricity substation in the Zhytomyr region and a cargo vessel in the Black Sea that it said was transporting military equipment and dual-use goods to Ukraine.

Kyiv Mayor Vitali Klitschko said Russian forces launched a missile attack on the capital early on Saturday. "Explosions in the Kyiv suburbs. The enemy is hitting infrastructure with ballistic missiles," he wrote on Telegram. Debris struck a five-storey apartment building in a western district of the capital, the facade of another residential building was damaged, and the city's military administration said a building housing a kindergarten was damaged.

Ukrainian officials said one person was killed in the Kyiv region and six people were injured in the capital, Kyiv Post reported. The Ukrainian air force said Russia launched 36 ballistic missiles and 172 drones in the overnight attack and that it had neutralised or suppressed four missiles and 149 drones by 09:00.

The attack came hours after President Volodymyr Zelenskiy warned in his nightly video address that Russia was preparing new strikes on energy targets, bridges across the Dnipro River and civilian infrastructure. It also came three days before US-led talks including Ukrainian and Russian negotiators open in Abu Dhabi on 10–11 November, with the Kremlin saying its delegation will include aide Vladimir Medinsky and military intelligence chief Igor Kostyukov, Reuters reported.
