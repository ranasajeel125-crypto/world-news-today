Virgin Media O2 to Cut Thousands of Jobs in Sweeping Shakeup, Sky News Reports

business · 31 Oct 2026 · 2 min read · By World News Today (Business Desk)

Virgin Media O2 is jointly owned by Liberty Global and Spain's Telefonica.

British telecoms operator Virgin Media O2, which is jointly owned by Liberty Global and Spain's Telefonica, will cut thousands of jobs in a sweeping shakeup, Sky News reported on Saturday, citing people close to the company.

Up to 4,000 of Virgin Media O2's 16,000 employees could be affected, although the precise number of job cuts has yet to be finalised, the report said.

Most of the job cuts are expected to affect non-customer-facing roles, while a number of senior leadership changes are already under way, the report said.

Virgin Media O2 did not immediately respond to a request for comment, Reuters reported.

The company is also expected to review its use of contractors and suppliers in an effort to reduce costs, the report said.

The Financial Times reported in July that Virgin Media O2's owners were weighing options to reduce the company's £22 billion ($29.46 billion) debt pile, Reuters noted. In September, the Financial Times reported that the company's shareholders were seeking to reduce costs by about £600 million.

Source: Reuters
