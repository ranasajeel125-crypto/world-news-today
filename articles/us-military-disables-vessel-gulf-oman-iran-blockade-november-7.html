US Military Fires on Vessel Trying to Break Iran Blockade in Gulf of Oman

world · 8 Nov 2026 · 3 min read · By World News Today (World Desk)

US Central Command said the vessel tried to leave regional waters after visiting an Iranian port; it estimates the months-long naval blockade has kept more than $25 billion of Iranian crude oil from being exported.

An American military aircraft fired on and disabled a commercial vessel that tried to break the US blockade of Iran on Saturday, US Central Command said.

The St. Kitts-flagged cargo vessel tried to depart regional waters after visiting an Iranian port, and a US plane fired precision munitions at its stern after US forces directed the crew to abandon the targeted spaces, Central Command said.

Building on the months-long naval blockade, the US military also said on Saturday it is launching a new task force to crack down on Tehran's movement of advanced weapons and other sanctioned material over its land borders and through the Caspian Sea.

Task Force Iron Gate, as it will be called, will be based in Tampa, Florida, and will coordinate with other US government agencies. Beyond the movement of weapons, it will also focus on dual-use goods and illegal revenue streams, Reuters reported, citing a statement from Central Command seen ahead of its release.

“Strengthening CENTCOM’s collaboration with US interagency partners will further constrain Iran’s ability to finance terrorism, rebuild weapons factories, and replenish their military,” Admiral Brad Cooper, the commander of Central Command, said in the statement.

Central Command estimated that its naval blockade has prevented Iran from exporting more than $25 billion in crude oil.

The incident comes as President Donald Trump has been threatening to carry out a new offensive against Iran, while Tehran has threatened broad retaliation against Gulf infrastructure, putting countries in the region on edge. It follows Iran's announcement on Saturday that it has lifted its self-imposed limit of about 2,000 km on ballistic missile ranges.
