China Blocks SoftBank's $5.4 Billion Takeover of ABB's Robotics Division

business · 5 Nov 2026 · 2 min read · By World News Today (Business Desk)

The original deal, announced in October 2025, would have put ABB's robotics business under Japanese ownership.

China has blocked SoftBank Group's $5.4 billion takeover of ABB's industrial robotics division, according to a statement released by ABB on November 5.

The Swiss firm said it had been informed by Japan's SoftBank that China's Office of the Working Mechanism for Foreign Investment Security Review has prohibited the acquisition of the ABB Robotics business in the country.

ABB added that both companies "are working constructively on the transaction". The original deal was announced in October 2025.
