Gunman Kills Six and Wounds 15 in Krakow's Main Market Square

world · 7 Nov 2026 · 3 min read · By World News Today (World Desk)

Police said the gunman drove onto the square at around 3 p.m. local time and fired at random people from his car; two police officers were among the injured.

Six people were killed and 15 injured when a gunman opened fire in the Main Market Square in Krakow, Poland, on Saturday afternoon, police said.

Krakow police said a man drove onto the square at around 3 p.m. local time and began firing at random people from his car. The suspect was wearing military-style clothing, police said, and was shot by officers before being taken to hospital.

Two police officers were among the injured, one of them in a critical condition, Poland's interior minister said, according to the BBC. A medical air rescue helicopter landed in the square as emergency services responded to the scene.

Early reports from the fire service, carried by TVN24, put the toll at two dead and at least 10 injured, Reuters reported. Police later confirmed five people had been killed; the toll rose to six after another victim died in hospital, police said. Four women and two men were among the dead.

Prime Minister Donald Tusk said police were investigating the “tragic shooting” and that he was being updated by the interior ministry. Interior Minister Marcin Kierwiński said everything indicated the gunman acted alone and held “very radical views.” Police said they were not looking for a second person and had not confirmed a motive.

Police said the investigation was continuing. Public shootings are rare in Poland, where gun laws are stricter than in the United States.

Source: Reuters — Two killed, at least 10 injured in shooting in Polish city of Krakow, TVN24 reports
