OpenAI’s Quarterly Revenue Tops $10 Billion for the First Time — But Losses Widen to $10.9 Billion

technology · 10 Nov 2026 · 3 min read · By World News Today (Technology Desk)

Chief Financial Officer Sarah Friar said the enterprise business doubled quarter over quarter and put OpenAI’s annual revenue run rate at roughly $50 billion.

OpenAI’s quarterly revenue rose about 50% to $10 billion in the third quarter, the Wall Street Journal reported on Tuesday, citing documents the company shared with investors — the first time the ChatGPT maker has crossed the $10 billion mark in a single quarter. Reuters reported the figures the same day.

The growth was driven by the enterprise business, which doubled quarter over quarter, Chief Financial Officer Sarah Friar said, with revenue now split roughly evenly between enterprise and consumer customers. Friar put OpenAI’s annual revenue run rate at roughly $50 billion.

The company is still far from profitable. Its operating loss widened to $10.9 billion in the quarter, from $10.1 billion in the second quarter, according to the documents reviewed by the Journal. Its adjusted operating margin improved to minus 109% from minus 150% — in plain terms, it lost about $1.09 for every $1 of revenue, against $1.50 a quarter earlier. The loss figures exclude stock-based compensation, which rose to $2.6 billion in the quarter.

A year earlier, in the third quarter of 2025, OpenAI reported $3.9 billion in sales, the documents showed — a measure of how fast its revenue has grown, and of how heavy the spending behind that growth remains.

The numbers arrive as investors scrutinise whether the artificial intelligence industry’s enormous spending can translate into profits. Rival Anthropic reported $17 billion in third-quarter revenue with a much smaller operating loss of $567 million, in an amended filing made on Tuesday ahead of its much-anticipated stock market listing, which could value it at about $1.4 trillion.

OpenAI, which is privately held and does not publish financial statements, did not immediately respond to a Reuters request for comment. The company also told investors it has raised $11 billion so far in a new funding round.
