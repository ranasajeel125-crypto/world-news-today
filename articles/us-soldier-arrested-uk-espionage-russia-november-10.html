Senior US Soldier Arrested in UK Over Alleged Espionage for Russia

world · 10 Nov 2026 · 2 min read · By World News Today (World Desk)

The 41-year-old was arrested on Tuesday, 10 November, and remains in custody at a London police station, as searches were carried out at two locations in Gloucestershire.

A senior member of the US Army has been arrested in the UK as part of an investigation by Counter Terrorism Policing, working closely with US authorities, the US military and the FBI, British police said on Tuesday.

The 41-year-old man was arrested near Gloucester on Tuesday, 10 November, on suspicion of offences under Section 1 and Section 3 of the National Security Act 2023. Officers are carrying out searches at two locations in Gloucestershire, and police said the country to which the allegations relate is Russia.

FBI Director Kash Patel said: “This important arrest involves allegations of espionage, which is of the utmost concern to the United States and our allies. While this investigation is continuing, I want to send a clear message – this FBI has no tolerance for anyone who breaks their oath to the United States and whose actions endanger our national security and that of our partners.”

Brig. Gen. Sean Stinchon, commanding general of the US Army Counterintelligence Command, said the alleged conduct of a senior soldier entrusted with sensitive information was “a sobering reminder that insider threats are real and persistent”.

Deputy Assistant Commissioner Vicki Evans, Senior National Coordinator for Counter Terrorism Policing, said: “This significant arrest and investigation is a result of close collaboration between UK and US authorities. The allegations are serious and our investigation is very much ongoing.”

The man has been detained under the National Security Act 2023 and remains in custody at a London police station. The investigation is being led by officers from Counter Terrorism Policing London and enquiries continue. Police said the investigation is not linked to any other ongoing Counter Terrorism Policing investigations. The BBC also reported the arrest on Tuesday evening.
