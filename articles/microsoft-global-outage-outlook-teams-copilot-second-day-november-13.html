Global Microsoft Outage Stretches Into Second Day as Fixes Fail to Take Hold

technology · 13 Nov 2026 · 3 min read · By World News Today (World Desk)

Outlook was the worst hit service, with reports peaking at about 130,000 on Thursday; schools, doctors' surgeries and businesses were among those affected.

A worldwide Microsoft outage that began on Thursday continued into a second day on Friday, leaving many users unable to access email and other services as the company said its attempts to fix the problem were not working as expected.

Services including Outlook, Microsoft 365, Teams, Copilot and OneDrive were affected. Outage tracker Downdetector recorded reports from 74 countries and territories, with reports about Outlook alone peaking at about 130,000 on Thursday afternoon. Around 15,000 Outlook problems were still being reported on Friday morning.

Microsoft said in updates on Friday that it was seeing improvement in some regions, including Europe and North America, but that its capacity had not yet fully returned to normal and some users could still face intermittent problems. The company said changes intended to improve service stability had not delivered the expected results, and that some servers brought back online had become unresponsive again under live traffic.

“We’ve monitored the status of the changes applied to improve serviceability and confirmed they’re not taking as expected. We’re identifying alternate workstreams that can be applied to increase capacity,” Microsoft said in a status update. The company said it was exploring alternative recovery measures and restricting some traffic to stabilise its infrastructure.

Microsoft said a process on affected servers responsible for establishing secure connections was becoming overwhelmed, causing the servers to stop processing requests, and that it had not confirmed a definitive root cause. It said there was no evidence of malicious activity associated with the incident, and it did not provide a confirmed timeline for full restoration.

Schools, doctors’ surgeries and businesses were among those hit by the disruption, the BBC reported, and OpenAI, the company behind ChatGPT, also reported issues after the outage began. Microsoft is tracking the broader Microsoft 365 incident under reference MO1550821 and the Exchange Online mailbox issue under EX1550543.
