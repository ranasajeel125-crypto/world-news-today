Typhoon Champi Disrupts Flights Across East Asia as It Nears Taiwan

world · 25 Oct 2026 · 3 min read · By Kenji Watanabe (Asia Correspondent)

Waves batter the coast as a typhoon approaches Taiwan. Airlines cancelled services across the region.

Typhoon Champi disrupted air travel across East Asia as the storm approached Taiwan, with several flights cancelled and passengers warned to expect further disruption as the system makes landfall.

Taiwan's weather authorities warned of destructive winds, torrential rain and dangerous coastal waves, and urged residents in exposed areas to secure property and avoid the shoreline. Ferries and some domestic transport services were also suspended as a precaution.

Champi is the latest in a season of powerful Pacific storms. Warmer ocean waters are giving typhoons more energy, and East Asian governments are investing heavily in flood defences, early-warning systems and evacuation planning to keep pace.

Airlines across the region said they would rebook affected passengers once conditions allow, and airports advised travellers to check flight status before leaving home.
