German Military Bans Chinese-Made Drones Over Security Concerns

world · 10 Nov 2026 · 2 min read · By World News Today (World Desk)

The drones were bought by individual units in recent years under a 2024 strategy that included a “white list” of approved systems — including several made by DJI, the world's largest drone maker — and are now being withdrawn as quickly as possible.

Germany's military has banned commercially available Chinese-made drones from its barracks and training grounds with immediate effect, over security concerns, Der Spiegel reported on Tuesday.

The drones, bought by individual military units in recent years with limited bureaucratic oversight, are being withdrawn from service as quickly as possible, the magazine reported. Germany's Defence Ministry was not immediately available for comment outside regular business hours.

Local commanders had been allowed to buy the drones under a 2024 strategy that included a “white list” of approved systems, including several made by China-based DJI, the world's largest drone maker, Der Spiegel reported.

Western military forces have grown increasingly concerned that Chinese-made technology could pose a security risk and may be used to transmit sensitive security information to Beijing.

As there is no central record of the purchases made by individual units, the military has had to impose a blanket ban, Der Spiegel added.

The United States last month prohibited vehicles made by China's BYD on its air base on Japan's Okinawa island, and Poland in February banned Chinese-made vehicles from military facilities over concerns their sensors could be used to collect sensitive data.
