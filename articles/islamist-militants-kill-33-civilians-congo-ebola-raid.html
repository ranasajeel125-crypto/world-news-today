Islamist Militants Kill at Least 33 Civilians in Congo Raid as Ebola Patients Flee

world · 6 Nov 2026 · 3 min read · By World News Today (World Desk)

The attackers set fire to a health post in Cantine-Aloyath, North Kivu, an Ebola hotspot; patients from a nearby hospital fled, including one confirmed Ebola case.

Islamic State-linked militants raided a village in eastern Democratic Republic of Congo, killing at least 33 civilians and forcing patients with confirmed and suspected Ebola infections to flee, authorities said on Friday.

The attackers, from the Allied Democratic Forces, a group recognised by Islamic State as an affiliate, struck Cantine-Aloyath, about 50 km west of Beni in North Kivu province, around 11 p.m. local time on Thursday, officials said. They set fire to a health post and several houses during the assault.

A total of 33 bodies were recovered after the attack, most bearing gunshot wounds, while others had been burned or hacked with machetes, according to Tchetche Aliamini, a local civil society leader. Beni territorial administrator Colonel Charles Omeonga told Reuters a death toll could not yet be established because heavy rain hampered army operations and investigations.

Patients from a nearby hospital also fled, including one confirmed Ebola patient and five suspected cases, among them three high-risk contacts, the head doctor there, Christian Kakule Vulembera, told Reuters.

North Kivu currently accounts for half of all new infections in Congo's Ebola outbreak. Authorities have recorded more than 10,000 cases and 5,000 deaths across the country.
