Israeli Strike on Gaza’s Nasser Hospital Kills Six, Including Surgeon

world · 12 Nov 2026 · 3 min read · By World News Today (World Desk)

Nasser Hospital in Khan Younis is one of the few functioning medical facilities left in Gaza; Reuters video showed extensive damage inside the surgery department.

An Israeli airstrike hit a building at Nasser Hospital in Khan Younis, in southern Gaza, early on Thursday, killing at least six people, including a surgeon, and wounding 20 others, a spokesman for Gaza’s civil defence service told Reuters.

Mahmoud Basal, the civil defence spokesman, said the strike hit the hospital’s surgery department and caused extensive damage. Medics identified the dead surgeon as Rezik Abushira. Reuters video from inside the hospital showed rubble and furniture strewn across rooms, with wires dangling from damaged ceilings.

The Israeli military later said it had targeted a senior Hamas militant and other members of the group’s armed wing who it said were operating inside the hospital. Reuters could not independently verify the Israeli military’s allegations.

Gaza’s health ministry gave the same death toll to the Associated Press and said a doctor was among the dead. A 13-year-old boy was also among those killed, according to the health ministry.

Nasser Hospital is one of the few functioning medical facilities left in Gaza, where the health system has been badly damaged by the war. The strike was reported amid a ceasefire that took effect in October 2025.
