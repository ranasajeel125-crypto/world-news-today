Democratic Socialist Nithya Raman Wins Los Angeles Mayor's Race, Ousting Karen Bass

politics · 6 Nov 2026 · 3 min read · By World News Today (World Desk)

Raman led Bass by 47,253 votes, 52.7% to 47.3%, in the latest count; Bass conceded on Thursday and promised a smooth transition.

Democratic socialist-backed candidate Nithya Raman has defeated incumbent Karen Bass in the race for mayor of Los Angeles, the Associated Press projected on Thursday, in an upset that puts a democratic socialist in charge of America’s second-largest city, Reuters reported.

The latest figures released on Thursday by the Los Angeles County Registrar-Recorder/County Clerk’s Office showed Raman, 45, a city council member, with 460,772 votes, or 52.7%, to Bass’s 413,519, or 47.3%. Bass, 73, conceded in a written statement, congratulating Raman and promising a smooth transition.

Raman is the first Los Angeles mayoral candidate to defeat an incumbent since 2005, when Antonio Villaraigosa unseated James Hahn. The India-born urban planner, an advocate for the homeless who first won a seat on the Los Angeles City Council in 2020, was once an ally of Bass and had initially endorsed her before announcing her own candidacy, saying the city had reached a “breaking point”.

Homelessness played a major role in the campaign. About 44,000 people were counted as homeless in Los Angeles in 2025, including about 27,000 living unsheltered on the streets, in vehicles or in encampments, according to the Los Angeles Homeless Services Authority. Bass, the first woman elected mayor of Los Angeles, had made fighting homelessness a top priority, but drew criticism from affordable-housing advocates who said she had made too little progress.

Bass’s standing was also undermined by criticism of the city’s response to the January 2025 Palisades fire, one of the deadliest and most destructive wildfires in the city’s history, which killed 12 people and destroyed more than 6,800 structures. Bass was out of the country when the fire ignited.

Raman has promised to triple homebuilding and to oust the city’s police chief, whom she views as too accommodating with federal immigration authorities. When she takes office in January, Los Angeles will become the nation’s second-largest city led by a democratic socialist, following New York, where Zohran Mamdani was elected mayor last year — meaning America’s two most populous cities will both be led by democratic socialists. Her win capped a Democratic sweep in the midterm elections in which the party also took control of both chambers of Congress.
