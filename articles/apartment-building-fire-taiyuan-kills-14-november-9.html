Apartment Building Fire in Taiyuan Kills 14

world · 9 Nov 2026 · 2 min read · By World News Today (World Desk)

Smoke billowed over the 40-storey building in downtown Taiyuan as firefighters on cranes battled the flames, in video verified by Reuters.

A fire in a high-rise apartment building in Taiyuan, the capital of China's northern Shanxi province, killed 14 people, the state-run Xinhua news agency said on Monday.

The blaze broke out at about 4:59 p.m. local time (0859 GMT) on Sunday in a building in the Liuxiang subdistrict of Yingze district, in the commercial heart of downtown Taiyuan. State media said the fire appeared to have started around the fourth floor of an annex before spreading to the eighth and ninth floors, which house a hotel. The 40-storey building is a mixed-use tower with flats, a hotel and children's education centres.

Five people had been confirmed dead by late on Sunday night, and the toll rose to 14 on Monday morning as people taken to hospital died of their injuries. Thirty-eight people were taken to hospital; 25 remained in hospital on Monday, nine of them seriously injured, state media said.

Firefighters brought the blaze under control after about two hours, and rescue operations were completed by 8:30 p.m. on Sunday. Video verified by Reuters showed smoke billowing over the 40-storey structure as firefighters poised on cranes outside battled flames spreading from the building's centre.

Authorities are investigating the cause of the fire, state media said. No cause has yet been given.
