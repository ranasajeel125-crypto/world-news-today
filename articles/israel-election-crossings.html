Israel to Close West Bank Crossings Ahead of Tuesday's Election

politics · 25 Oct 2026 · 2 min read · By Maria Santos (Europe & Middle East Correspondent)

Israel votes on Tuesday 27 October, and ahead of the ballot the army is closing West Bank crossings and restricting the movement of Palestinian workers, according to BBC News reporting.

Closures around Israeli elections are a long-standing security practice, but they carry a heavy economic cost for Palestinian workers who cross daily into Israel, and for the businesses on both sides that depend on them.

The vote comes amid an escalating crackdown in the West Bank, and the result will set the direction of Israeli policy — and the temperature of the region — for the years ahead.

Source: BBC News reporting, via the 25 October world brief
