Powerful Earthquake Strikes China's Tibet Region

world · 25 Oct 2026 · 2 min read · By Kenji Watanabe (Asia Correspondent)

A powerful earthquake has struck China's Tibet region, prompting emergency response efforts and raising concerns about communities in one of the most remote and mountainous areas on Earth.

Rescue teams were dispatched to assess damage and reach affected villages, where landslides and damaged roads can isolate settlements quickly. Earthquakes in the Himalayan belt are a persistent threat: the region sits on the collision zone between the Indian and Eurasian plates.

Authorities said assessments of casualties and damage were continuing, with emergency shelters and supplies being moved into the area as aftershock warnings remained in place.
