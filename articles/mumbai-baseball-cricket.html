Yankees Beat Dodgers in Mumbai as Baseball Meets Cricket

sport · 25 Oct 2026 · 3 min read · By Ravi Menon (Sport Desk)

Mumbai's cricket fans packed the stands at the Andheri Sports Complex on Saturday to watch Indian World Cup-winning captain Suryakumar Yadav swing a bat — just not the kind they are used to.

Indian cricketers Suryakumar Yadav, Shardul Thakur, Deepak Chahar and Radha Yadav lined up alongside ex-Major League Baseball players Nick Swisher, Adrian Gonzalez, Jake Arrieta and Jonny Gomes in a three-on-three tournament pitched as a bridge between the two bat-and-ball games. Each team mixed a baseball veteran, an Indian cricketer and an American softball player, with scoring that rewarded both muscle and precision: two points for a home run, four for hitting the target zone, one for a catch, and totals doubled by five consecutive out-of-the-park hits.

The semifinals offered a thriller and a stroll, as Gonzalez's Los Angeles Dodgers overturned the Shardul-led Chicago Cubs' 44 runs with a minute to spare, while Suryakumar's New York Yankees cruised past the Boston Red Sox. That set up a rematch of the 2024 World Series — this time in India — with the Yankees posting 78 and holding off the Dodgers to be crowned champions. Softball player Rachel Garcia was named the tournament's most valuable player.

Suryakumar, who guided India to victory at this year's T20 World Cup on home soil, said he is confident baseball will grow in India: 'You must be strong from head to your lower body, have a good core and strong arms. If you want to hit 450 feet, 500 feet, you got to be strong. Work hard. No substitutes.'
