At Least 21 Dead After Migrant Boat Sinks Off Senegal Coast

world · 8 Nov 2026 · 2 min read · By World News Today (World Desk)

The boat sank off Kebemer, about 180 km north of Dakar; the ministry did not say how many people were on board.

At least 21 people have died after a boat carrying migrants sank off the coast of Senegal, the country's interior ministry said on Sunday.

Authorities had rescued one survivor and recovered 21 bodies as of Saturday evening, the ministry said in a statement on X. The accident occurred off Kebemer, a town about 180 km north of the capital, Dakar, and search and rescue operations were continuing as authorities looked for possible additional survivors.

The boat was carrying people seeking to migrate irregularly, the statement said. It did not say how many people were on board, so the final death toll could still rise.

Thousands of people have died in recent years attempting the Atlantic crossing from West Africa to Spain's Canary Islands, including 635 in the first five months of this year, according to migrant rights group Walking Borders.
