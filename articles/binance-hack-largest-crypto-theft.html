Binance Hit in Largest Crypto Hack on Record as $3 Billion Frozen

business · 30 Oct 2026 · 2 min read · By World News Today (Business Desk)

Blockchain analysts estimate about $5.9 billion was taken — nearly four times the previous record — while Binance says customer losses will be covered.

Binance, the world's largest cryptocurrency exchange by trading volume, was hit on Thursday in what analysts say is the largest crypto theft on record, with an estimated $5.9 billion drained from its hot wallets, according to blockchain analytics firm Chainalysis.

The attacker targeted two Binance hot wallets, known as Binance 14 and Binance 16, and moved bitcoin, ether and stablecoins across several blockchains, on-chain data showed. Security researchers said the attacker exploited an Ethereum feature that let a wallet hand control to a piece of software, turning it into a sweeper that forwarded incoming funds to the attacker's own addresses.

Binance said its security teams were investigating the breach and that user funds are safe. Co-chief executive Yi He said on X that the platform would cover all hacking losses, and that withdrawals were suspended until its environment is confirmed safe.

Stablecoin issuers moved quickly to freeze part of the haul: Tether said it froze more than $1.567 billion in USDT, Circle froze around $823 million in USDC, and Ripple froze about $200 million. Binance said on Friday that the verified total frozen had reached $3 billion and could rise further as investigators trace additional assets. The final loss remains undetermined.

At an estimated $5.9 billion, the theft would be nearly four times the $1.5 billion Bybit exploit of February 2025, the previous record. The hack rattled crypto markets, driving down bitcoin and other digital assets, Reuters reported on Friday, and also weighed on shares of rival exchange Coinbase.

Investigators including ZachXBT and security firm Blockaid have suggested North Korea was likely behind the attack, though that attribution has not been officially confirmed. Binance said it would provide updates through its official channels as the investigation continues.
