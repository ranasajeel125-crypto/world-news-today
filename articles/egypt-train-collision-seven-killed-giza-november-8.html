At Least Seven Killed and 112 Injured in Egypt Train Collision

world · 8 Nov 2026 · 2 min read · By World News Today (World Desk)

At least seven people have been killed and 112 injured after two passenger trains collided south of Cairo on Sunday, Egypt's health ministry said.

The state railway authority said a train travelling from Giza to Fayoum hit the back of another heading from Cairo to Aswan, derailing several carriages. The collision happened in al-Ayyat district, Giza province, about 76 km (47 miles) south of the capital.

At least 52 ambulances raced to the scene, the health ministry said, and the train's conductor was among those killed, according to the chief prosecutor's office. Authorities said the death toll could still rise as rescuers searched the wreckage.

President Abdel Fattah al-Sisi ordered a swift investigation and said anyone found negligent should be held accountable. Egypt's public prosecutor said investigations were under way to determine the technical reasons behind the crash.

Emergency crews were clearing the wreckage to restore services on the Cairo-Upper Egypt line, the railway authority said, without saying how long that would take.

Al-Ayyat district was also the scene of Egypt's worst-ever rail disaster.
