Varma's Blistering Fifty Powers India to Series Lead Over New Zealand

sport · 24 Oct 2026 · 3 min read · By Ravi Menon (Sport Desk)

India chased 181 with a ball to spare in Christchurch to take a 2–0 series lead.

India's middle-order batter Tilak Varma hammered a half-century off 25 balls as India chased down a target of 181 with a ball to spare, taking a 2–0 lead in the five-match Twenty20 series with New Zealand on Saturday.

New Zealand captain Mitchell Santner's destructive spell of 4–21 had left India shaken, with 68 runs needed from the last five overs. But Varma (51) and Nitish Kumar Reddy (26) targeted the pacers in the final three overs, adding 69 runs off 30 balls for the seventh wicket to secure a four-wicket win.

'The Hyderabadi boys were in play today… what everyone believed, I have done it, so I'm really proud of myself,' said player of the match Varma.

Earlier, the Christchurch pitch looked slower than in Thursday's high-scoring contest as India's pacers restricted New Zealand to 43–3 during the powerplay. Glenn Phillips (70) and Bevon Jacobs (58) turned the innings around, adding 116 for the fourth wicket. Phillips hit four boundaries and four sixes before Arshdeep Singh (2–33) had him caught near the fence, and Michael Bracewell's 10 off two balls helped the hosts reach 180–5 — a total that proved just short.
