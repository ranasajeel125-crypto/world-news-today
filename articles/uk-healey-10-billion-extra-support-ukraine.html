Britain to Give Extra £10 Billion of Support to Ukraine, Healey Says

world · 28 Oct 2026 · 2 min read · By World News Today (Europe Desk)

Chancellor John Healey announced the package in Wednesday's Budget, saying it would be based on loans, export finance guarantees and urgent military funding for the winter ahead.

British finance minister John Healey said on Wednesday that Britain would provide £10 billion ($13.29 billion) of additional economic and military support to Ukraine to help in its war with Russia.

Healey told lawmakers the £10 billion would be "based on loans, export finance guarantees and (£500 million) in urgent military funding for this winter."

A Downing Street spokesperson said in a statement that the £10 billion would be made up of £7 billion of economic support over three years and £3 billion in export finance capacity for Ukraine.

The spokesperson added that the £500 million of military funding would "plug urgent gaps in Ukraine's air defences" as winter approaches.

Healey also said that he and Prime Minister Andy Burnham had spoken to Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskiy before the budget and that Zelenskiy "told us 'you've made my day'".

The announcement comes as Ukraine heads into another winter of war, with air defences under strain from Russian strikes, and as European allies weigh how to sustain support for Kyiv alongside the diplomatic push for talks.
