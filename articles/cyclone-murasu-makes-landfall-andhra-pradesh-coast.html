Cyclone Murasu Makes Landfall on India's Eastern Coast

world · 3 Nov 2026 · 2 min read · By World News Today (World Desk)

The storm's centre was expected to cross the coast near Ongole by about 4 a.m. local time, with winds of 100–110 km/h gusting to 120 km/h.

Severe Cyclonic Storm Murasu began making landfall along India's eastern coast early on Tuesday, the country's weather department said, the second storm to hit the region in a week as authorities evacuated thousands of people to safety.

The India Meteorological Department said the severe cyclonic storm started making landfall at 1:00 a.m. local time (1930 GMT on Monday) over the coast of the south-eastern state of Andhra Pradesh. The landfall process takes a few hours, with the storm's centre expected to cross the coast between Nellore and Narsapur, close to Ongole, by about 4 a.m. local time, with winds of 100–110 km/h gusting to 120 km/h.

Authorities began evacuating thousands of people on Monday and planned to move some 30,000 to safety in Andhra Pradesh, which is in the path of the cyclone, Reuters reported.

Cyclone Murasu formed over the Bay of Bengal, a major region for tropical cyclones that has spawned deadly storms on India's east coast. IMD forecasts said the states of Andhra Pradesh and Tamil Nadu to its south would be worst hit.

At least six people were killed in Cyclone Arnab, which was classified by IMD as a very severe cyclonic storm and made landfall in Andhra Pradesh on Wednesday, Reuters reported — making Murasu the second cyclone to strike the state's coast within a single week.
