Steel Plant Explosion in Turkey Kills 16

world · 9 Nov 2026 · 3 min read · By World News Today (World Desk)

Smoke rose over the HABAS plant in Aliaga after the blast, in footage shown on Turkish television; witnesses said the ground shook.

A huge explosion and fire at an iron and steelworks in western Turkey killed 16 people on Monday, officials said, in one of the country's deadliest industrial accidents in years.

The blast struck the HABAS plant in Aliaga, in Izmir province about 60 kilometres north of the port city of Izmir, at around noon local time (0900 GMT), the provincial governor's office said. President Recep Tayyip Erdogan said 13 people were injured, two of them critically, in a televised address after a cabinet meeting.

Provincial Governor Suleyman Elban said an overhead crane fell into a furnace as it was replacing a boiler, causing an explosion in a nearby steel mill. About 200 workers were in the area instead of the usual 300 because production had been halted for the boiler switch, he said.

The death toll climbed through the day, from an initial three dead, 14 injured and 10 missing. The governor later put the toll at 10 dead and 15 injured, with four workers still missing, while Erdogan's figure of 16 dead and 13 injured has not been reconciled with the on-site count. Izmir Mayor Cemil Tugay said the final toll would only be known once the debris was cleared.

“The ground shook. It was very intense,” witness Nuri Ali Ulker was quoted as saying by the state-run Anadolu news agency. Another witness, Deniz Ulker, said the explosion “felt like the earth’s crust was shifting beneath us. I thought it was an earthquake.”

Interior Minister Mustafa Ciftci said emergency teams fought the blaze while searching for casualties, and the injured were taken to hospital. Justice Minister Akin Gurlek said four public prosecutors had been assigned to the case, and the private Demiroren News Agency reported that five people had been detained in relation to the explosion.

“Judicial and administrative investigations have been launched into the explosion,” Erdogan said. “The reasons and circumstances of this tragic event, which has deeply saddened our nation, as well as whose negligence was involved and who is responsible, will be revealed.”

The HABAS plant, established in 1987, can produce 4.5 million tons of steel a year, according to the company's website. Aliaga, a large port and industrial district on Turkey's Aegean coast, is also known for shipbreaking.
