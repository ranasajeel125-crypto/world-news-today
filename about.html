About World News Today

World News Today is an independent world news blog. Our small newsroom follows the stories shaping politics, business, technology, sport and the climate — and explains, in plain language, why they matter to readers everywhere.

Every story on this site is reported from established international news wires and official sources, which are credited at the foot of each article. Our blog desk adds analysis, explainers and opinion on top of the day's reporting.

Reporting sources we follow and credit: Reuters, BBC News, Al Jazeera, the Financial Times, the New York Post, TechCrunch, Carbon Pulse, and official releases from agencies such as NASA, the US Department of Energy, the IMF and India's Ministry of Science & Technology.

Sections we publish: World, Politics, Business, Technology, Sport, Climate and the Blog.

News tips and corrections: newsroom@worldnewstoday.news
