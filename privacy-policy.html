Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

World News Today is an independent world news blog. This policy explains what information is collected when you visit this site, how cookies and third-party advertising work here, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

This site does not ask you to create an account, and we do not directly collect your name, email address, or other personal details when you read articles. If you email us, we receive the information you choose to include in that message, and we use it only to reply to you.

Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs for security and operation. These logs can include technical details such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages you visit. We do not use server logs to identify individual readers.

Cookies and third-party advertising

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep the site working and to show advertising. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet. You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalised advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info/choices.

Other third-party advertising providers may also place cookies or web beacons on your browser to measure ad performance. Their use of cookies is governed by their own privacy policies.

Links to other websites

Our articles link to reporting sources such as Reuters, BBC News, and Al Jazeera. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their practices.

Children's privacy

This site is a general news site and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it was last changed.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, email us at newsroom@worldnewstoday.news.
